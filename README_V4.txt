My Buddy V4 – Monthly History + Month End Report

New in V4:
- Monthly history (Jan, Feb, Mar… based on recorded transactions)
- Dedicated Month End Report screen
- Expense-category pie chart
- Monthly money-flow bar chart
- Income, spending, investments and balance summary
- Month selector
- Largest expense category and basic month-end observations
